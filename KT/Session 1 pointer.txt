Slide 9: Logging & Monitoring Tips

Cloud Logging

STS automatically writes transfer job logs to Cloud Logging.

Filter logs by resource.type=storagetransfer_job to see details.


Airflow Task Logging

Use logging.info() in DAG tasks to record job name, run_id, and status.

Prefer structured JSON logs for easier parsing and alerts.


Pub/Sub Notifications

Configure notificationConfig in STS job to push SUCCESS/FAILURE events.

Subscribe Airflow DAGs or Cloud Functions to capture and react.


Airflow Sensors

Use CloudDataTransferServiceJobStatusSensor to wait for completion.

Automatically retries or fails DAG based on STS outcome.


XComs for Traceability

Push job details (job_name, operation_id, status) to XCom.

Downstream tasks can use these for reporting or conditional branching.


Monitoring Dashboards

Export metrics (bytes transferred, job failures) to Cloud Monitoring.

Build dashboards and alerts for visibility of job health.


Error Handling

Wrap STS operators with try/except → log exceptions to Cloud Logging.

Set Airflow retries & alerting policies (Slack, Email).
