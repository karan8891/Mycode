Here’s the quick, no-fluff rundown.

CF2 (orchestrator / mock of DAG1–3)

Trigger: Pub/Sub topic that CF1 publishes to.

Reads: CF1 message → grabs sparkflow_parms and cdm_object_mapping[0].

Validates/loads: From SOR bucket it opens:

<object>_sor_config_file.json

<object>_sor_db_conn.json


Extracts: Source details (DB type/name/table/query) + connection bits (driver/user/JCEKS/URL).

Mocks DAGs: Creates three JSON status files:

dag1_ingest.json (pretend RAW land)

dag2_quality.json (mock checks)

dag3_publish.json (pretend curate/publish)


Writes: All artifacts under
runs/<cdm_process_id>/<object_name>/ in the same SOR bucket, plus a verification.json with parsed summaries.

Optional: If NEXT_TOPIC is set (or hardcoded), publishes a small summary (with run_root) to CF3’s topic.

No BQ writes (per your instruction).


CF3 (completion / audit sink)

Trigger: Pub/Sub topic that CF2 publishes to (the NEXT_TOPIC one).

Reads: CF2 summary (ideally includes run_root and sor_bucket).

Verifies: Checks that the four files exist in the SOR bucket path:

verification.json, dag1_ingest.json, dag2_quality.json, dag3_publish.json


Writes: A final cf3_done.json with COMPLETE (all present) or PARTIAL (some missing).

BigQuery audit: Adds 3 rows (CF2 unchanged):

1. CF3_RECEIVED — when message arrives


2. CF3_VERIFIED — after checking which files are present/missing


3. CF3_DONE — after writing cf3_done.json (status = COMPLETE/PARTIAL)
(Enabled when BQ_AUDIT_DATASET + BQ_AUDIT_TABLE env vars are set; otherwise it skips gracefully.)




That’s it: CF2 validates and mocks, CF3 verifies and finalizes—with your 3 BQ entries handled entirely in CF3.
